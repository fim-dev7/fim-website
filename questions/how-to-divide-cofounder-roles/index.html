More from this episode

In the closing game, Andy revealed a clean division of labour with his co-founder. Asked whether he'd rather give up music or beer for the rest of his life, he said he'd give up music, only because he could make enough music to entertain himself — and "I can make better music than I can beer I'm not a Brewer."

That's Benny's job — Benny is the professional brewer. As Andy puts it: "I work on the brand he brings the beer / and that's a good match." It's all about co-founder synergies.

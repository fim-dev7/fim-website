Show notes ↗

MaiMoney is a fintech platform in Vietnam that gives everyday investors access to wholesale investment products and the technology to grow their wealth. Nhi's framing: "we want to bridge the gap by making investing more simple, as simple as savings" while providing significantly higher returns.

More from this episode

Nhi, the CEO, says traditional savings accounts in Vietnam offer a lower return but high security, while investment products are either too complex or not accessible for the everyday Vietnamese investor. MaiMoney bridges that gap by making investing as simple as savings and providing significantly higher returns to help retail investors build their wealth. It does this by partnering with licensed fund managers who manage the investments, while MaiMoney provides the tech and infrastructure — handling onboarding, customer servicing, and even sales and marketing for the fund manager products. At the time of the conversation the platform had reached $2 million in investments and was growing 45% month over month.

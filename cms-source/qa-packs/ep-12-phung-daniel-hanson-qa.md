# How did SipHRD get started?

slug: how-siphrd-started

answer: It started at a party two years ago. Phung and Daniel were "sitting down about 10 20 shots in drinking" and decided "we should start a business" — and alcohol came into play almost immediately, since both had travelled overseas and seen alcohols not available in the Australian market.

Phung describes the origin: all three were at a party, she and Daniel were having a "little D&M session," and "10 20 shots in" they decided to start a business and started "feeding off each other" discussing options. Both had gone overseas and "seen a lot of different alcohols that's not in the Australian market," and they wanted to bring those into Australia. A couple of days later, Daniel messaged Phung "with like details on how to start," and she knew he was "fully serious."

The recipe was the bottleneck. # How should I price a premium consumer product?

answer: SipHRD ran "a lot of competitive analysis," comparing RTD hard seltzers on alcohol percentage and volume, then priced "slightly higher if not on par" — because they "find that our drink does have a lot more quality" in ingredients, can and carton.

Daniel, "the finance guy," handled pricing.

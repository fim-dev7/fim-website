> **SEO note:** Most directory links are `nofollow` (Apple, Spotify, Amazon, iHeart, Overcast, Pocket Casts, YouTube Music). The two worth prioritising for link value are **Goodpods** and **Podchaser** — both expose a do-follow link to your website *once you claim and verify the show*. An unclaimed listing won't give you the link, so claiming is the step that matters.

---

### 1. Overcast
- **URL:** https://overcast.fm/add
- **Account:** Not required.
- **Steps:**
  1. Overcast pulls from the Apple directory, so since you're on Apple it should appear in search within 1–3 days automatically.
  2. To force it, paste the RSS feed into the **Feed URL** field at the link above and click **Add podcast**.
  3. Allow up to 2–3 days to surface. No claiming or profile control.

### 2. Pocket Casts
- **URL:** https://pocketcasts.com/submit
- **Account:** Not required.
- **Steps:**
  1. Paste the RSS feed into the submission field.
  2. Tick the **public** box so the show appears in search and Discover for everyone.
  3. Submit. Indexing takes ~12 hours; full directory listing can take a few days.

### 3. Goodpods — *claim for the do-follow link*
- **URLs:** Add at the creator dashboard → **Add a Podcast**; claim at https://goodpods.com/claim-podcast
- **Account:** Yes — create/sign in to claim.
- **Steps:**
  1. Goodpods is powered by Apple, so the show likely already exists there — search for "Founders In Motion" first.
  2. Go to **Claim a Podcast** (or the URL above), find the show, tick the box certifying ownership, and click **Claim your podcast**.
  3. Once claimed, edit the profile and add `https://foundersinmotion.tech` — this is rendered as a **do-follow** link.

### 4. Podchaser — *claim for the do-follow link*
- **URLs:** Add at https://www.podchaser.com/add ; claim at https://www.podchaser.com/claim
- **Account:** Yes — needed to add and claim.
- **Steps:**
  1. At `/add`, paste the RSS feed (or an Apple/Spotify link) to pull in the show and all episodes.
  2. On the show page, click **Claim This Podcast**, then verify ownership — choose **Verify via email** (sent to the address in your feed) or **Verify via feed** (paste a token into the RSS).
  3. After claiming, set a custom URL and add `https://foundersinmotion.tech` to the profile — rendered as a **do-follow** link. Claiming also lets you reply to reviews and add badges.

### 5. Amazon Music / Audible
- **URL:** https://podcasters.amazon.com
- **Account:** Yes — sign in with any Amazon account.
- **Steps:**
  1. Click **Get Started**, sign in, and paste the RSS feed.
  2. Select your primary audience country, confirm the cover art on the "Is this your podcast?" screen, and accept the Terms.
  3. Click **Confirm Email** and follow the confirmation email link. Live within ~24 hours on both Amazon Music and Audible.

### 6. iHeartRadio
- **URL:** https://podcasters.iheart.com
- **Account:** Yes — log in or create a free iHeartRadio account.
- **Region note:** Submitter must be located in the US, Canada, Mexico, Australia, or New Zealand — fine for Thea (Australia).
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign in and enter the RSS feed URL.
  2. iHeart validates ownership using the email visible in the feed — make sure that address is correct and monitored.
  3. Submit; approval is manual and can take a few days to a couple of weeks.

### 7. YouTube Music
- **URL:** https://studio.youtube.com → **Content** → **Podcasts** (or the listener-side library add)
- **Account:** Yes — Google/YouTube account.
- **Heads-up:** FiM already publishes video episodes on YouTube (@foundersinmotion). YouTube Music will surface that existing video channel as the podcast — you generally do **not** also feed it the audio RSS, or you'll create a duplicate show. Only use the RSS path if you want a separate audio-only listing.
- **Steps (audio RSS path, only if wanted):**
  1. In YouTube Studio, go to **Content → Podcasts**, open **Details**, and under **RSS settings** click **Connect to RSS feed**.
  2. Accept the terms, paste the RSS feed, and enter the verification code YouTube emails to the address in your feed.
  3. Choose whether to import the full back catalogue or only new episodes; episodes import as **private** first — review, then publish to public.

---

**Suggested order of attack:** Goodpods and Podchaser first (claim them for the backlinks), then Amazon Music and iHeartRadio (manual, biggest audiences), then Pocket Casts and Overcast (quick paste-and-go). Decide the YouTube Music question deliberately given the existing video channel.

### 3. Step-by-step: how to create the item

1. Go to **wikidata.org** and **log in** (create a free account if you don't have one — use your real-ish handle; brand-new accounts can create items immediately).
2. Left sidebar → **"Create a new Item"** (or go directly to `wikidata.org/wiki/Special:NewItem`).
3. Fill in **Language = English**, **Label = `Founders In Motion`**, **Description = `podcast hosted by Thea Ngo featuring interviews with startup founders`** → click **Create**.
4. On the new item page, find the **"Also known as"** field and add each alias from the table above (press Enter between each).
5. Scroll to **Statements** → click **"+ add statement"**. Type the property name (e.g. "instance of"), select it, then type the value (e.g. "podcast") and pick the matching blue suggestion. Click **publish**.
6. Repeat step 5 for each row in the property tables — start with **instance of**, **official website**, **inception**, **language**, then the four platform IDs.
7. For each statement, click **"add reference"** underneath it and add `reference URL` + `retrieved` so it survives editor review.
8. (Optional, recommended) Create the **Thea Ngo** person item the same way, then return and add the host statement linking to it.
9. Save. Within hours to a few days the item is live and crawlable; Google may take a few weeks to surface it in the Knowledge Graph.

One caution: Wikidata is community-edited, so keep everything factual, sourced, and non-promotional. Marketing language ("the best founder podcast") will get reverted — the neutral wording above is deliberately safe.

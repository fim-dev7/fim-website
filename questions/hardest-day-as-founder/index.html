More from this episode

Nam says he could answer simply by saying every day is hard. The one that stands out: when they first began fundraising their pre-seed, "it was like January in Poland." It was cold, fundraising outside San Francisco "is just not ideal," and they were "getting a lot of nose back to back." He was close to a deadline — his parents had said that if nothing happened by then, he'd have to go back to school. To get through it he relied on three abstractions he gave himself: whether the work aligned with his core values, the fact that he genuinely enjoys solving hard problems, and that he got to solve them with his co-founder, someone he really enjoyed working with.

# Hook
Most 25 year olds are figuring out their first job — Finnlay Morcombe built Fluency, convinced the investors behind Facebook to lead a $6 million seed round before he'd even unpacked in San Francisco, and hard pivoted within one week of going to market.

# Story
Most 25 year olds are figuring out their first job. Finnlay Morcombe built Fluency, a platform that maps how work actually happens in Fortune 500 organizations.

The product was built by self taught devs from Melbourne. When they went to market with it, the read came fast: within one week they were "like okay, we are hard pivoting."

Around the same stretch, Finnlay convinced the investors behind Facebook to lead a $6 million dollar seed round — before he'd even unpacked in San Francisco.

The mindset that runs underneath it all: "we would rather company die trying than be a $50 million dollar company." It's the kind of conviction that frames the whole conversation — that and an open question Finnlay keeps circling back to about where all of this is headed: "agency is so powerful, what's gonna happen?"
